ABOUT THE COLLEGE
Karan Arts and Science College is a co-educational institution located at Velu Nagar, Su Kilnachipattu, Thenmathur, Tiruvannamalai, Tamil Nadu - 606603.

The college website states that the institution was started by the Saraswathi Ammal Educational Trust and was established in 2017.

VISION
To provide excellent higher quality education with leadership qualities, moral values, training and research.

MISSION
To improve infrastructure facilities, develop trained human resources, enhance leadership qualities among youth, and promote patriotic and ethical values.
